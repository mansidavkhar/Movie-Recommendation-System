BEHIND THE REEL

How it works

We find viewers who rated your movie above 4 stars, count which other movies those viewers also loved, and compare that share with the broader MovieLens audience. That makes popular-but-obvious movies less dominant and surfaces distinctive matches.

Your searched film is always excluded from its own recommendations.
